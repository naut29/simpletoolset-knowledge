## FAQs

- Does compression reduce quality?: Compression re-encodes page images. Lower quality settings reduce file size more but may soften images.
- Will text-based PDFs get smaller?: Compression is most effective on scanned or image-heavy PDFs. Text-heavy PDFs may not shrink as much.
