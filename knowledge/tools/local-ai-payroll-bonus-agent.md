# Local AI Payroll & Bonus Agent - Private Excel Payroll Logic in Your Browser

## Overview

Read local attendance or payroll workbooks, turn spoken salary rules into spreadsheet logic, and export a processed payroll file privately in your browser.

Reads attendance or payroll workbooks in the browser, interprets plain-language salary and bonus rules with a local Phi-3-mini style workflow, applies the generated logic to each row, and exports a processed workbook without app-server upload.

This tool is available in multiple languages on SimpleToolSet (EN, VI, ZH, KO, JA, TH, DE, PT-BR, ES-419, ID).

## Key features

- SheetJS workbook parsing and export inside the browser
- Local Phi-3-mini style payroll rule interpretation with no app-server workbook upload
- Generated payroll columns such as bonus, deduction, net pay, and notes
- Row-by-row workbook processing plus a result summary sheet
- Quick-start templates for common bonus and penalty policies

## How to use

1. Upload an XLSX, XLS, or CSV payroll workbook from your device
2. Choose the sheet you want to process
3. Describe lateness, KPI, overtime, bonus, or deduction rules in plain language
4. Optionally start from a ready-made payroll template
5. Review the generated columns, totals, and preview rows before downloading the result workbook

## FAQs

- Does the workbook get uploaded to the app server?: No. Workbook parsing, rule drafting, row processing, and export happen in the browser. Model assets may download on the first run, but the payroll workbook itself is not uploaded to the app server.
- What kinds of rules can it handle?: It is meant for plain-language payroll rules such as lateness deductions, KPI bonuses, overtime additions, attendance rewards, and similar spreadsheet-friendly salary logic.
- Does it replace payroll compliance review?: No. It drafts and applies local workbook logic, but payroll, tax, labor, and HR compliance still need human review before use.
- Can I start from a template instead of writing every rule from scratch?: Yes. The tool includes quick templates for common bonus and penalty policies so you can adapt them rather than start with a blank prompt.
