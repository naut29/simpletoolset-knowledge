# In-Browser Video Transcoder - Private FFmpeg Video Converter

## Overview

Convert local video files with FFmpeg.wasm in your browser without uploading them to a server.

Transcode local video files into MP4, WebM, or MKV with browser-side FFmpeg processing, adjustable quality presets, and optional audio removal.

This tool is available in multiple languages on SimpleToolSet (EN, VI, ZH, KO, JA, TH, DE, PT-BR, ES-419, ID).

## Key features

- Private FFmpeg.wasm transcoding in the browser
- MP4, WebM, and MKV output support
- Quality presets for smaller files or higher fidelity
- Automatic multi-thread mode when SharedArrayBuffer is available

## How to use

1. Upload a local video file
2. Choose the output format, quality preset, and resolution target
3. Keep or remove audio depending on the final use case
4. Start transcoding and download the converted file locally

## FAQs

- Does the video leave my device?: No. The transcoding workflow runs locally in your browser and does not upload the file to a server.
