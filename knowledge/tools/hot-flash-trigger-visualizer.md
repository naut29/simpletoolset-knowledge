# Hot Flash Trigger Visualizer - Private Local Hot Flash Tracker

## Overview

Track hot flashes and review likely triggers like caffeine, stress, heat, and spicy food locally in a private browser journal.

Logs hot flash timing, intensity, suspected triggers, and related context in a private browser journal with local charts and on-device pattern review.

This tool is available in multiple languages on SimpleToolSet (EN, VI, ZH, KO, JA, TH, DE, PT-BR, ES-419, ID).

## Key features

- IndexedDB storage for private hot flash records
- Offline-first route with scoped service worker support
- Local Chart.js dashboards for symptom intensity and trigger frequency
- Tracks caffeine, stress, heat, spicy food, timing, notes, and pattern clusters
- JSON backup and restore without accounts

## How to use

1. Log the date, time, and intensity of each hot flash episode
2. Tag likely triggers such as caffeine, stress, heat, or spicy food
3. Add context notes about sleep, activity, room temperature, or schedule changes
4. Review local charts for trigger frequency and symptom trends
5. Export or import your private JSON backup when needed

## FAQs

- Does this confirm the medical cause of hot flashes?: No. It helps you identify personal repeating patterns, but it does not confirm a medical diagnosis or cause.
- Where is my trigger data stored?: Entries are stored locally in your browser with IndexedDB and can be exported as your own JSON backup.
- Can it work offline?: Yes. It is designed as an offline-first route once the required assets have loaded in your browser.
- Are caffeine, stress, heat, and spicy food analyzed in the browser only?: Yes. Trigger analysis stays on-device in your browser and is not sent to the app server.
- When should I seek medical care instead of only tracking symptoms?: If a hot flash happens with chest pain, fainting, shortness of breath, or other unusual symptoms, seek prompt medical evaluation.
