# Audio Stem Splitter - Local AI Vocal Remover & Music Splitter

## Overview

Split vocals, instrumental, drums, bass, and other stems locally in your browser with on-device AI.

Separate a local audio track into vocals, instrumental, drums, bass, and other stems with browser-based AI running on your device.

This tool is available in multiple languages on SimpleToolSet (EN, VI, ZH, KO, JA, TH, DE, PT-BR, ES-419, ID).

## Key features

- Local AI stem separation with no audio upload
- Vocals, instrumental, drums, bass, and other stem outputs
- WebGPU-preferred processing with WASM fallback
- WAV exports plus one-click ZIP download for all stems

## How to use

1. Upload a local audio file from your device
2. Choose whether to prefer WebGPU or stay on WASM
3. Start stem separation and wait for the model to process the track locally
4. Preview and download vocals, instrumental, or the full ZIP package

## FAQs

- Does the audio leave my device?: No. The audio file stays on your device while the separation model runs locally in your browser.
