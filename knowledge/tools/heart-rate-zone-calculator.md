# Heart Rate Zone Calculator - Training Zones

## Overview

Estimate training zones from age-based maximum heart rate.

This calculator estimates maximum heart rate using 220 minus age and breaks it into common training zones: warm-up, fat burn, cardio, and peak.

This tool is available in multiple languages on SimpleToolset (EN, VI, ZH, KO, JA, TH, DE, PT-BR, ES-419, ID, HI, BN, TA, TE, MR).
