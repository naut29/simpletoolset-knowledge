# AI Background Blur for Sensitive Photos - Free Online Tool

## Overview

Blur the background or detected subject in sensitive photos locally with browser-side AI segmentation.

Detect the main person in a photo with browser-side AI segmentation, then blur the background or the detected subject region locally without uploading the image to a server.

This tool is available in multiple languages on SimpleToolset (EN, VI, ZH, KO, JA, TH, DE, PT-BR, ES-419, ID, HI, BN, TA, TE, MR).

## Key features

- Browser-side person segmentation with MediaPipe Selfie Segmentation
- Blur background or detected subject region
- Adjustable blur strength
- Segmentation mask preview for quick review
- No image upload to the app server

## How to use

1. Upload a local photo
2. Choose whether to blur the background or the detected subject
3. Pick the segmentation model and blur strength
4. Run the AI blur and review the result
5. Download the processed PNG

## FAQs

- Does this blur faces only?: Not exactly. The tool segments a person region, which is useful for privacy editing, but it is not a guaranteed face-only blur detector.
- Is my image uploaded?: No. The blur workflow runs locally in your browser and the image is not sent to the app server.
- Can I blur the background instead of the person?: Yes. You can choose to blur the background or the detected subject region.
