# Menopause Doctor Visit Summary - Create a Private Visit PDF Offline

## Overview

Create a private menopause or perimenopause visit summary and export a local PDF for doctor conversations.

Organizes menopause or perimenopause symptom notes, visit questions, medications, and dated symptom history in a private browser draft, then exports a local PDF summary for doctor visits.

This tool is available in multiple languages on SimpleToolSet (EN, VI, ZH, KO, JA, TH, DE, PT-BR, ES-419, ID).

## Key features

- IndexedDB storage for a private doctor-visit draft
- Local PDF export with jsPDF and no file upload
- Scoped service worker for an offline-first route
- Structured symptom history with dates, severity, and frequency
- Local JSON backup import and export

## How to use

1. Add your visit date, menopause stage, period pattern, and main concerns
2. List medications, supplements, and questions you want to raise
3. Log dated symptom history entries with severity, frequency, and notes
4. Review the local summary and export a PDF directly on your device
5. Keep a private JSON backup only if you want your own local copy

## FAQs

- Does this tool create the PDF locally?: Yes. The PDF is generated directly on your device and is not uploaded during export.
- Where is the draft stored?: The draft is stored locally in your browser with IndexedDB unless you export your own backup.
- Is this a medical record or medical conclusion?: No. It is only a conversation aid for doctor visits, not an official record or medical conclusion.
- Can I use it offline?: Yes. It is designed as an offline-first route once the required assets have loaded in your browser.
- What if my symptoms feel unusual or severe?: A summary tool does not replace medical care. Concerning or unusual symptoms should be evaluated by a qualified clinician.
