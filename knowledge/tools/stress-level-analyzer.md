# Stress Level Analyzer - Daily Stress Score

## Overview

Get a quick stress score and zone from everyday lifestyle signals.

This tool estimates a daily stress score from common lifestyle inputs. It uses simple scoring rules to classify stress into a zone and suggest targeted tips.

This tool is available in multiple languages on SimpleToolSet (EN, VI, ZH, KO, JA, TH, DE, PT-BR, ES-419, ID).

## Key features

- 0-100 stress score
- Low/Moderate/High zone labels
- Actionable tips based on inputs
- Deterministic, client-side calculation

## How to use

1. Enter sleep hours, work hours, and screen time
2. Add caffeine intake and exercise minutes
3. Review the stress score and zone
4. Use the tips to adjust your routine

## FAQs

- Is this a medical diagnosis?: No. It is a self-reflection tool with simple heuristics.
- Does it store my data?: No. All calculations run in your browser.
