## FAQs

- How are volume units converted to grams?: Volume conversions assume water density: 1 ml = 1 g, with tsp = 5 ml, tbsp = 15 ml, and US cup = 240 ml.
- Does this work for flour or sugar?: The volume-to-mass conversion is for water only. Ingredients like flour or sugar have different densities.
