# Mood & Brain Fog Tracker - Private Local Menopause Journal

## Overview

Track mood swings, anxiety, and brain fog locally in a private menopause journal.

Logs mood, anxiety, brain fog, focus, energy, sleep quality, and likely triggers in a private browser journal with local charts and offline-first storage.

This tool is available in multiple languages on SimpleToolSet (EN, VI, ZH, KO, JA, TH, DE, PT-BR, ES-419, ID).

## Key features

- IndexedDB storage for private journal entries
- Offline-first route with scoped service worker support
- Local Chart.js dashboards for mood, anxiety, and brain fog patterns
- Tracks focus, energy, sleep quality, panic episodes, and trigger tags
- JSON backup and restore without accounts

## How to use

1. Log the date and rate mood, anxiety, brain fog, focus, energy, and sleep quality
2. Mark whether mood swings, panic, or strong daytime impact were present
3. Tag likely triggers such as stress, poor sleep, hot flashes, or skipped meals
4. Review recent trend charts and common trigger frequencies
5. Export or import your private JSON backup when needed

## FAQs

- Does this diagnose anxiety or depression?: No. It is a private tracking and reflection tool, not a mental health diagnostic tool.
- Where is my journal stored?: Entries are stored locally in your browser with IndexedDB and can be exported as your own JSON backup.
- Can it work offline?: Yes. It is designed as an offline-first route once the required assets have loaded in your browser.
- What if I have self-harm thoughts or severe panic?: Those symptoms need urgent medical or crisis support rather than self-tracking alone.
