# Daily Calorie Needs Calculator (TDEE)

## Overview

Use TDEE to estimate maintenance calories based on your activity level.

This calculator estimates total daily energy expenditure (TDEE) by multiplying BMR with an activity factor. Enter age, height, weight, gender, and activity level to get an estimated maintenance calorie range.

This tool is available in multiple languages on SimpleToolset (EN, VI, ZH, KO, JA, TH, DE, PT-BR, ES-419, ID, HI, BN, TA, TE, MR).

## Key features

- BMR + activity factor model
- Multiple activity levels
- Clear maintenance calorie estimate
- No data storage

## How to use

1. Enter age, height, weight, and gender
2. Select your activity level
3. View BMR and TDEE results
4. Copy results for planning

## FAQs

- What is TDEE?: TDEE is the estimated total calories your body uses in a day including activity.
- Which activity level should I choose?: Pick the level that best matches your typical weekly activity or training.
- Is Daily Calorie Needs (TDEE) free to use?: Yes. It is a free online tool and does not require signup.
