Tired of managing multiple PDF files? Our PDF merger combines all your documents into one organized file, making sharing and archiving simple.

Combine multiple PDF documents into a single file. Upload your PDFs, arrange them in your preferred order, and merge them into one comprehensive document. Perfect for combining reports, consolidating documents, or creating document bundles.

1. Upload multiple PDF files by clicking or dragging

## FAQs

- How many PDFs can I merge at once?: There's no hard limit on the number of files. However, merging many large PDFs may take longer and require more memory.
- Will the merge affect PDF quality?: No, merging preserves the original quality of each PDF. Text, images, and formatting remain exactly as they were in the source files.
- Can I merge password-protected PDFs?: No, password-protected PDFs cannot be merged directly. You'll need to unlock them first using appropriate software.
- Does the order I upload matter?: Yes, PDFs appear in the merged document in the order you arrange them. Use the reorder buttons to arrange them correctly before merging.
