# Pregnancy Due Date Calculator - LMP to Due Date

## Overview

Estimate due date and trimester ranges from LMP with a simple calculation.

This calculator estimates due date by adding 280 days to the last menstrual period (LMP). It also outlines trimester date ranges for quick planning.

This tool is available in multiple languages on SimpleToolSet (EN, VI, ZH, KO, JA, TH, DE, PT-BR, ES-419, ID).

## Key features

- LMP-based due date
- Trimester breakdown
- Clear date outputs
- Runs entirely in your browser

## How to use

1. Select the first day of your last menstrual period
2. View the estimated due date
3. Review trimester date ranges
4. Copy results if needed

## FAQs
