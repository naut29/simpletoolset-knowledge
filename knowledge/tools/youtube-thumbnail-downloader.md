- Why is maxres not available for some videos?: Not all videos have maximum resolution thumbnails. The tool will show all available resolutions for each video.
- Does this work with private videos?: It only works with thumbnail URLs that are publicly accessible. Do not use it to bypass private, age-restricted, removed, or otherwise restricted content.
