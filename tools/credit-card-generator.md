## FAQs

- Are these real credit cards?: No. The numbers are generated for testing only and cannot be used for real transactions.
- Will these work on live payment gateways?: No. Use official test cards provided by your payment processor for live sandbox testing.
- What is the Luhn check?: The Luhn algorithm is a checksum used to validate card numbers. Generated numbers pass this check.
- Is it legal to use these numbers?: Yes, for testing and validation only. Do not use them to attempt real payments.
