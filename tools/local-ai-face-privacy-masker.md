# Local AI Face Privacy Masker - Private Face Blur in Your Browser

## Overview

Automatically blur or cover faces in photos locally in your browser with a private MediaPipe Face Mesh workflow.

Detect visible faces in photos directly in your browser with a local MediaPipe Face Mesh workflow, then blur or fully cover those face regions so you can anonymize images without uploading the source file to the app server.

This tool is available in multiple languages on SimpleToolset (EN, VI, ZH, KO, JA, TH, DE, PT-BR, ES-419, ID, HI, BN, TA, TE, MR).

## Key features

- Private MediaPipe Face Mesh face detection in the browser
- Blur and solid privacy-mask output modes
- Adjustable coverage around each detected face
- No app-server upload for the source image
- Offline-friendly route and reusable cached app assets

## How to use

1. Upload a JPG, PNG, WebP, or another supported image from your device
2. Choose whether detected faces should be blurred or fully covered
3. Pick a tighter or wider coverage area around each detected face
4. Run local face masking in the browser
5. Review the protected image and download the masked PNG

## FAQs

- Does this upload my photo to the app server?: No. The image stays in the browser during face detection and masking. The app server does not receive the source photo.
- Can I choose between blur and full blocking?: Yes. You can either blur detected faces or place a solid cover over them, depending on how strong the privacy protection needs to be.
- Will it find every face perfectly?: No. It works best on clear, visible faces and should be reviewed before you publish or share the final image.
- Does it work offline?: The route is built with offline support and bundled runtime assets. After the page assets are cached, reopening the tool can be more reliable offline depending on browser cache state.
