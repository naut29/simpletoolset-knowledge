# Menopause Symptom Tracker - Private Local Menopause Journal

## Overview

Track hot flashes and broader menopause symptoms together in a private local journal without account or uploads.

Logs menopause or perimenopause symptoms such as hot flashes, night sweats, sleep disruption, mood changes, brain fog, joint pain, and daily impact in a private browser journal with local charts and on-device trend review.

This tool is available in multiple languages on SimpleToolset (EN, VI, ZH, KO, JA, TH, DE, PT-BR, ES-419, ID, HI, BN, TA, TE, MR).

## Key features

- IndexedDB storage for private symptom records
- Offline-first route with a scoped service worker
- Local Chart.js dashboards for symptom trends and tag frequency
- Tracks hot flashes and broader menopause symptom patterns in one place
- JSON backup and restore with no uploads

## How to use

1. Log the date and rate daily symptoms such as hot flashes, night sweats, and sleep disruption
2. Record mood changes, brain fog, joint pain, energy, and daytime impact
3. Tag related symptoms like irregular periods, anxiety, vaginal dryness, or headaches when relevant
4. Review local trend charts for symptom intensity and repeated patterns
5. Export or import your private JSON backup without using an account

## FAQs

- Does this diagnose menopause?: No. It is a private symptom-tracking tool for personal trends and does not diagnose menopause.
- Where is my data stored?: Entries are stored locally in your browser with IndexedDB and can be exported as your own JSON backup.
- Can it work offline?: Yes. It is designed as an offline-first route once the required assets have loaded in your browser.
- Do I need an account?: No. It works entirely in the browser without accounts, cloud sync, or data uploads.
- When should I seek medical care instead of only tracking symptoms?: If symptoms strongly affect sleep, mood, or daily life, or suddenly become much worse, seek medical evaluation.
