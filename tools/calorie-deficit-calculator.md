# Calorie Deficit Calculator - Daily Calorie Change

## Overview

Estimate the daily calorie change needed to reach a weight goal.

This calculator estimates the daily calorie change needed to lose or gain a target amount of weight over a chosen time period. It uses 1 kg 7700 kcal as the energy estimate.

This tool is available in multiple languages on SimpleToolset (EN, VI, ZH, KO, JA, TH, DE, PT-BR, ES-419, ID, HI, BN, TA, TE, MR).

## Key features

- Lose or gain goals
- Daily and total calorie change
- Simple 7700 kcal per kg model
- Fast, private output

## How to use

1. Select lose or gain goal
2. Enter target weight change
3. Enter number of weeks
4. Review daily and total calorie change

## FAQs

- Is 7700 kcal per kg exact?: It is a common estimate and may not match individual outcomes.
- Is this medical advice?: No. This tool provides a simple estimate only.
- Is Calorie Deficit Calculator free to use?: Yes. It is a free online tool and does not require signup.
